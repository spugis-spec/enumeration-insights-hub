🆕 New / Not-Yet-Onboarded Enumerators

For enumerators who've joined but don't have a username yet. Set a usual work plan (manual entry, or "shadow" any onboarded enumerator from any group they worked alongside) and add day-specific overrides for dates they worked with someone different. They'll show up clearly flagged in their group's report, but their numbers are never added to the totals — until you click "Onboard" and give them a real username, after which their real uploaded data counts normally from the next report. Onboarding now saves the new mapping automatically. You can also set or change their usual partner right from the Dashboard table each day it's generated — leave "just this date" unchecked and it becomes their standing default going forward; check it to only affect that one date. Their full work history (numbers and who they worked with, every day) is kept permanently under 📜 Staff History above, even after onboarding or removal.

Every add / edit / remove here is saved to the database automatically — not saved yet this session. Use the button below any time you want to force-sync or confirm it went through.

⏪ Backfill a Missed Staff into Saved Dates

Use this when someone was left out of the enumerator mapping. With the full data files uploaded, this adds ONLY that person's numbers to each already-saved dashboard in the date range. Every other row, remark, status and total stays exactly as saved — if the person is already present on a date, that date is skipped untouched.

🎯 Weekly Performance vs Target

Target: 600 points / month · ≈30 captures / working day · 150 / 5-day week

🔐 Admin authorisation required to edit Outcome & Remark

Rules: unreported days are ignored. Excused / Sick days are skipped (no penalty). Absent days are counted as 0 so poor attendance shows. Days with a 0 total whose status or remark mentions an ISSUE (e.g. LOGISTICS ISSUE) are excluded from the computation. Each staff target = daily target × the number of days actually counted for that staff.
